MOCK INTERNAL DOCUMENT: written for the proof of concept, not a Nan Fung record. Example Court is fictional.

## The deal

A 1980s City Fringe office of 60,000 sq ft, refurbished to Grade A.

## What happened

- Facade costs ran 18% over budget.
- Letting the space took 14 months.

## Lessons

- Allow a 20% contingency on refurbishment budgets for 1980s buildings.
- Pre-let at least 30% of the space before committing to a full refurbishment.
