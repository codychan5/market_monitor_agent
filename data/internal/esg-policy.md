MOCK INTERNAL DOCUMENT: written for the proof of concept, not a Nan Fung record.

## Energy

- Every owned office reaches EPC B before 2031.
- Major refurbishments target EPC A.

## Carbon

- Prefer refurbishment to demolition.
- Schemes over 50,000 sq ft need a whole-life carbon assessment before investment-committee approval.

## Certification

- BREEAM Excellent on major refurbishments.
- A NABERS UK rating of at least 4.5 stars on new Grade A space.

## Reporting

Report energy use and EPC ratings for every asset each year.
