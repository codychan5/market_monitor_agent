MOCK INTERNAL DOCUMENT: written for the proof of concept, not a Nan Fung record. Example House and its owner are fictional.

## Proposal

Buy Example House, a 45,000 sq ft City Core office, from Example Office Holdings Limited for £68m.

## Decision

Declined on 14 March 2024.

## Reasons

- The asking price was above our replacement-cost estimate of about £1,250 per sq ft.
- The building is rated EPC F; reaching EPC B would cost about £11m.
- The main tenant, paying 62% of the rent, leaves in 2026.

## Revisit if

The owner is forced to sell. The owner bought the building in 2018 with a five-year bank loan, since extended to 2025; if the loan is not refinanced, a sale below replacement cost becomes likely.
