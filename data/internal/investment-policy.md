## Scope

London offices in City Core, City Fringe, King's Cross/Euston and Midtown.

## What we buy

- Buildings of 30,000 to 150,000 sq ft.
- Value-add refurbishments, development with pre-lets, and core-plus.
- Distressed or forced sales below replacement cost.

## Hard rules

- No purchase rated below EPC C without a costed plan to reach EPC B before 2031.
- No single-let building with under three years left on its lease, unless the price assumes the building is empty.
- At most 50% loan-to-value at purchase.
- Every bid over £50m needs investment-committee approval.

## Return targets

- Core-plus: 8% unlevered IRR.
- Value-add and development: 12% unlevered IRR.

## Out of scope

Canary Wharf, homes, retail and logistics.
