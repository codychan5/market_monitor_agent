MOCK INTERNAL DOCUMENT: written for the proof of concept, not a Nan Fung record.

## 1. Confirm the insolvency

Check the Gazette notice and Companies House: who the administrator is, and the date of appointment.

## 2. The lender and the loan

Find the lender, when the loan matures and the loan-to-value. A loan taken out in 2017 or 2018 for five years is a priority.

## 3. The building

Size, grade, EPC rating, and the cost of bringing it to Grade A and EPC B.

## 4. The leases

Average unexpired lease term, leases ending within three years, and the main tenants.

## 5. Price

Compare the likely price with replacement cost and recent City sales.

## 6. Fit

Check the investment policy's hard rules before spending more time.

## 7. Next step

Call the administrator and ask for the sale timetable and the data room.
