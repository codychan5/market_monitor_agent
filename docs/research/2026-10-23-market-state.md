## 2. Key themes 2025–2026

- **Grade A shortage, refurbishment over new build:** no ground-up schemes started in Q3, refurbishments were 91% of Big Six starts, and 2027–28 deliveries run 32% below average (Savills, 19 Oct). Deloitte flags a 2027–30 supply gap ([28 Apr](https://www.deloitte.com/uk/en/about/press-room/deloitte-london-office-crane-survey-april-2026.html)).
- **Record rents:** 77 Grosvenor St let at £201 psf (Q1, Savills). One Leadenhall's top floor reportedly let at £160 psf, but the rent wasn't disclosed ([CoStar, Feb](https://www.costar.com/article/39103580/brookfield-letting-shatters-city-of-londons-record-office-rent)).
- **AI firms are the new demand:**
  - They leased over 450k sq ft in April, against a 2025 average of 40k a month, with almost half in King's Cross ([CoStar via Resultsense, 14 May](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-05-14-london-ai-office-space-tenfold-rise/)).
  - KF records Anthropic taking 158k sq ft at 1 Triton Sq and Microsoft 94k at Film House.
- **MEES/EPC decision** ([GOV.UK interim response, updated 18 Jun 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/non-domestic-private-rented-sector-minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-epc-b-implementation/outcome/minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-mees-in-the-non-domestic-private-rented-sector-interim-response)):
  - The 2027 EPC C milestone is dropped.
  - **EPC B applies from 2031** (not 2030), only to privately rented buildings **over 1,000 m²** in England and Wales, and only "where cost effective".
  - Smaller buildings stay at EPC E.
  - It is not yet law: it needs secondary legislation, and there is no timetable. The regulatory detail is still pending.
- **Attendance:** UK office occupancy has settled above 40%, with a record 44.1% in January, against 60–80% before Covid. Banks are pushing five-day mandates ([Remit, 26 Feb](https://return.remitconsulting.com/resource-centre/57-uk-office-occupancy-stabilises-above-40-as-sector-divide-widens)). I found no London-only figure.
- **Canary Wharf:**
  - Barclays bought One Churchill Place for £750m at 5.62%.
  - Canary Wharf Group sold 1–5 Bank St for £625m to its own owners, Brookfield and QIA ([Bloomberg, 13 Aug](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/canary-wharf-sells-societe-generale-office-to-its-own-investors)). Because buyer and seller are related, it is weak price evidence.
  - HSBC leaves 8 Canada Sq in 2027. Plans have been filed to retrofit it into offices plus a 181-room hotel, estimated at £0.8–1bn ([Construction Enquirer, 13 Aug](https://www.constructionenquirer.com/2026/08/13/plans-in-for-striking-hsbc-canary-wharf-tower-revamp/)).
- **Repricing:** KF records 1 America Sq selling for £44m (12.84% yield, £190 psf) and 123 Victoria St at 8.23%. Hammersmith's Metro Building was sold by receivers for about £20m ([CoStar, Feb](https://www.costar.com/article/113122832/arora-buys-hammersmith-offices-from-receivers-for-circa-20-million)).
- **Refinancing:** UK commercial property debt stands at £174bn, with £33bn maturing in 2026. 13% of tested loans have interest cover below 1x ([Bayes, May](https://www.bayes.citystgeorges.ac.uk/news-and-events/news/2026/may/sluggish-investment-in-uk-commercial-real-estate-market-sparks-price-war-for-refinancing-loans)\*).
- **Bank of England:**
  - Four 2025 cuts took Bank Rate to 3.75% on [18 Dec](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/boeapps/database/Bank-Rate.asp), and it has been held all of 2026.
  - At the September meeting the vote was 6–3, with three members wanting a rise.
  - CPI is 3.1% and is forecast above 4% in Q1 2027 because of the Middle East energy shock ([BoE, 17 Sep](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy-summary-and-minutes/2026/september-2026)). The next decision is 5 Nov.
  - KF links the Q3 rise in yields to rate volatility.
- **Asian and Hong Kong capital:**
  - Hong Kong owners are still net sellers (Colliers\*). Wing Tai sold 1 Savile Row for £31m ([Jun](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/wing-tai-properties-sells-london-savile-row-asset-for-gbp31-million)).
  - Far East Consortium's 40 Furnival St was repriced from £18m to £12m ([Jun](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/amtd-agrees-acquire-london-office-tower-17-mln)).
  - Japan is the active Asian buyer (£617m in 2025\*; [Savills, 13 Mar](https://www.savills.co.uk/blog/article/388845/commercial-property/japanese-capital-and-central-london-offices--a-measured-return--not-a-sudden-rush.aspx)). Singaporean capital is waiting for cheaper debt.

## 3. Nan Fung's London exposure

- **Platform:** Nan Fung has held a majority stake in Endurance Land, its UK platform, since 2018 ([Mingtiandi](https://www.mingtiandi.com/real-estate/outbound-investment/nan-fung-buys-majority-stake-in-uks-endurance-land/)).
- **Listed assets:** [nanfung.com](https://www.nanfung.com/en/property/international/) lists five:
  - 99 City Road
  - Regent Quarter, King's Cross
  - 108 Cannon St (38,800 sq ft, bought 2016)
  - 138 Cheapside (2016, about £75–83.5m)
  - 16 Old Bailey (2015, about £77m)
- **Regent Quarter:**
  - Bought from ADIA/LaSalle in December 2017 for £302.75m ([CoStar](https://www.costar.com/article/187632/nan-fung-exchanges-on-300m-regent-quarter-acquisition)).
  - It won a planning resolution in 2023 for a life-science-led redevelopment ([CoStar](https://costar.com/article/1286179960/endurance-land-and-nan-fung-can-pivot-to-life-sciences-in-kings-cross)).
  - **OpenAI** took 88,500 sq ft there ([Ashurst, 14 Apr 2026](https://www.ashurstperkinscoie.com/en/who-we-are/our-news-work-market-recognition/ashurst-advises-endurance-land-on-major-openai-letting-at-kings-cross/)). KF records the Jahn Court part at £97 psf blended.
- **99 City Road:**
  - A 36-storey, 475k sq ft tower, with Multiplex appointed as main contractor ([Endurance Land, 10 Aug](https://www.enduranceland.com/2026/endurance-land-and-multiplex-advance-landmark-tower-99-city-road/)).
  - Construction starts Q3 2026, with completion in 2030 (slipped from H1 2029).
  - Knight Frank is the letting agent. Funding is undisclosed.
- **Not Nan Fung:** 50 Bank St (2014) was bought by Vivien Chen's personal vehicle ([WealthBriefing Asia](https://wealthbriefingasia.com/article.php/Hong-Kong-Family-Buys-Prestigious-Office-Development-In-UK%27s-Canary-Wharf)).
- **Disposals:** I found none in 2024–26, so current holdings need confirming internally.
- **Demo hooks:**
  - 99 City Road completes into the forecast supply gap.
  - Regent Quarter sits in the AI cluster, where King's Cross vacancy is 4.0%.
  - The City assets over 1,000 m² fall under the 2031 EPC B rule.

**Gaps an in-house agent could fill:**
- Reconcile several brokers' figures and show the spread, not one number.
- Screen deals and news against Nan Fung's investment strategy.
- Run an internal opportunity feed that ties market events to owned assets: comparable lettings, MEES exposure, sales at yields above 8%, receiverships, and Hong Kong family disposals as off-market leads.
- Brief in both English and Chinese (I found no vendor doing this; unverified).

**Licensing risk (flag for legal):** Knight Frank's PDFs state that "use of our data on AI platforms… is strictly prohibited". The demo should use licensed feeds (Green Street or MSCI connectors, CoStar) or get written permission.
